# Imported reference introductions

Records marked `import_method: "wikipedia-category-library"` contain introductory text reproduced from Wikipedia. Whitespace is normalized, and card descriptions may be shortened with an ellipsis. Each record carries the original page URL, revision ID, contributor attribution, access time, and license. Where available, Indonesian and English introductions come from their respective language editions; they are not presented as translations of one another.

Credit: **Wikipedia contributors**. Consult `sources` in each record for the precise page and revision. Source article history links identify the individual contributors. The repository's software license does not override this content license. Reuse and adaptations of this text must retain attribution and follow the applicable share-alike terms.

Wikidata labels, descriptions, and identifiers are available under **CC0**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Editorial reading guides, legacy records, documentary image licenses, and generated illustrations are distinguished from imported source text. Documentary images retain the licenses listed in their individual image records. Generated illustrations are identified in `assets/art/README.md`.

## Coverage and uncertainty

The bulk library contains source introductions, not individually reviewed scholarly dossiers. Category membership and Wikidata identity support discovery and deduplication; they do not establish every claim in a source article. Source-language gaps, missing images, unspecified dates, and unassessed abilities remain explicit. The import report records failed requests and excluded topics. Empty fields must not be replaced with invented lore, automatic power rankings, or fabricated translation.
